Our Drivers

Patient, encouraging driving mentors.

“There is never any rush. We go at your speed until driving feels simple, natural, and safe.”

Northland Driving is an independent, woman-owned local business based in Gladstone, Missouri. Mary leads our team of patient female driving instructors, specializing in teaching adult women how to drive with patience, kindness, and practical neighborhood experience.

As lead instructor, Mary and our team understand how daunting it can be to master a new life skill in a new environment or language. We offer lessons in both English and Tagalog, ensuring every explanation is crystal clear and every lesson is completely judgment-free.

Genuine Patience

No yelling, no pressure. Mary and our team explain every step clearly until you feel 100% comfortable.

Car & Free Pick-up Included

Practice in our clean, safe vehicles. Free pick-up & drop-off across the KC Northland.

English & Tagalog

Fluent, warm instruction in English or Tagalog so you learn in your preferred language.
