About

Calm instruction, from someone who takes the time.

“No rush. We go at your pace until the car feels normal.”

Northland Driving is a small, woman-owned local business based in Gladstone. Instruction is one-on-one and always in our car, so all you have to do is show up.

She has taught many people to drive and has real patience for it. As an immigrant, she knows what it's like to learn something big in a new country and language, so lessons can be taught in English or Tagalog, without any rush.
