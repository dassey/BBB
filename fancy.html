About the House

The J.C. Wyatt House is a meticulously restored 1891 residence offering a singular fine-dining experience. Guests enjoy attentive service, a quiet setting, and a menu crafted to celebrate seasonality and classic technique.

  • Authentic Victorian ambiance across intimate dining rooms
  • Multi-course menus with thoughtful wine options
  • Reservation-only service to preserve privacy and pace
